02What this policy covers

This policy covers the Moto Ceramic mobile app for Android and iOS, and the booking records it creates. It does not cover any third-party site you reach from a link in the app, including the payment page described in section 6, which is governed by that provider’s own policy.

04What we deliberately do not collect

Stated plainly, because these are the things people reasonably assume an app of this kind takes:

  • Your device’s location. The app never asks for location permission and never reads GPS. Every address we hold is one you typed in yourself.
  • Your card, UPI ID or bank details. These are entered on the payment provider’s own page and never reach our servers. We receive only a transaction reference and whether it succeeded.
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files. The app requests none of these permissions.
  • Advertising identifiers, or any form of behavioural or cross-app tracking. The app contains no analytics or advertising software of any kind.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

05Why we collect it

We process your data to deliver a service you asked for: to create and manage your account, price and schedule your booking, send a technician to your address, take payment, keep you informed about your booking, and meet our tax and accounting obligations.

Where the law requires your consent, you give it by creating an account and making a booking, and you can withdraw it as described in section 9. The optional fields — email, gender, notifications — are genuinely optional, and declining them does not stop you booking.

07Where your data is stored

Your account and booking data is stored in India, in Supabase’s Mumbai region (ap-south-1).

Our other providers may process data outside India in the course of delivering messages and payments. Where that happens, it is limited to the data listed against that provider in section 6.

08How long we keep it

We keep your account and its booking history for as long as your account is open, so that you can see what work has been done on your vehicle.

If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data within 180 days, except payment and invoice records, which we must retain for 8 years under Indian tax law.

09Your rights

You may:

  • See what we hold. Your name, phone, email, address and full booking history are visible in the app under Profile, and you can ask us for a copy of anything else.
  • Correct it. Profile details can be edited in the app at any time. For anything else, contact us.
  • Delete it. Ask us to close your account and erase your data, subject to the retention exception in section 8.
  • Withdraw consent. Turn notifications off in your device settings, or ask us to stop processing your data — which will mean closing your account, since we cannot deliver a booking without it.
  • Complain. To our Grievance Officer below, and after that to the Data Protection Board of India.

We will respond to any of these within 21 days, and we do not charge for it.

10Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

11How we protect your data

Access is restricted at the database level, so one customer’s account cannot read or alter another’s. Your login session is held in your device’s encrypted secure storage. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit, and payment credentials never pass through our systems at all.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

12Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and change the “last updated” date above. Where a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
